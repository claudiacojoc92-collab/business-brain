Decide what your marketing should do next.

Business Brain is a marketing decision system for founder-led businesses. It reads your business, tells you what it understood, proposes one strategy for you to adopt or challenge, and turns it into a 30-day plan with a move for today.

Who it's for

Founders who run the business and its marketing themselves: studios, clinics, practices, small service businesses and independent brands. You have a website, an Instagram account, a brochure or an offer document, and more ideas than time. You don't have a marketing team to tell you which one to do first.

Marketing is the first area Business Brain works on.

What it reads

You add what you already have. Business Brain reads all of it together, as one picture of the business.

  • Your website. Add the address and it reads the pages.
  • Your Instagram. Connect a professional (Business or Creator) account and it reads your profile and your recent posts.
  • Your documents. Upload PDF, Word or text files: an offer, a brochure, a price list, a case study.
  • Any link. Paste a page that matters: a press piece, a review page, a competitor.
  • Your own words. Describe the business in a few sentences if there's nothing written down yet.

It keeps what it saw in your sources separate from what you told it, and it says so when it is inferring rather than reading.

How it works: four moments

1. Understanding

Business Brain tells you what it read, what stood out, and what it will ask you about. It points out where your sources disagree with each other, for example an offer on your website that your brochure prices differently, and it shows which source each point comes from.

2. Conversation

It asks you a few questions about what the sources can't show: what you want from the next months, who you most want to reach, and what you won't do. You can correct anything it got wrong.

3. Strategy

It proposes one marketing bet, with what it chooses over the alternatives, the trade-offs, what to leave for later, and what would make it worth reconsidering. You adopt it or challenge it. Nothing is adopted for you.

4. Plan

Once you adopt a strategy, it becomes a 30-day plan. Each time you come back, Business Brain shows you the move for today.

What it makes

  • An understanding of your business, grounded in your own sources.
  • One adopted marketing strategy, written down with its trade-offs.
  • A 30-day plan and a move for today.
  • Draft copy for the moves, such as a landing page, written from what your sources actually say.

Everything it writes for your business is in your business's language.
