# Intent: Result Loop V1 (referral acquisition)

- **Status:** approved (build gated on `approve migration`)
- **Requested by:** operator, 2026-10-14, after the ground-truth audit (https://claude.ai/artifact/N58TNcLq442bb2Z9FKiw6X)
- **Founder #0:** Body Move Studio (Cluj): goal = more medical referrals.

## Problem
BB stops at a good decision. After Today there is no system: no contact, no touch history, no typed outcome,
no follow-up clock; outcome reports are free text filed under a model-chosen founder_state kind (default
"constraint"); the verdict UI can say Today changed and unchanged at once; the email is built from the plan's
first action with no sender, no recipient and no founder writing, so it reads as corporate AI prose.

## What we build
Close ONE loop end to end: STRATEGY → CONTACT → OUTREACH → FOLLOW-UP → OUTCOME → IMPACT → NEXT MOVE.
BB owns the reasoning, the operating state around the decision, the writing it can do, the tracking, the outcome
interpretation and the next adaptation. The founder owns the commercial result. No claim that BB "produces customers".

## Not in scope
Full CRM, Gmail send/OAuth, Instagram publishing, SEO, brochure engine, a Systems page, execution marketplace,
pipeline dashboard, new IA, landing redesign. The uncommitted Strategic Moves work stays as it is (not wired here).

## Must not be rewritten
Understanding + provenance, corrections, held/versioned strategy + adopt/challenge, readiness engine, Today,
the impact evaluator (extended, not replaced), the execution handoff, frozen Voice/content slices, asset lineage.
